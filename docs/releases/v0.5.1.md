## What's changed

- **Android setup shows what your device can do.** The last setup step shows whether this device can connect headphones and let go of them, and whether switching is confirmed on it.
- **Adding headphones shows where they're already set up.** In the list, headphones you've already added, or added on another of your devices, are marked, so you pick the right pair.
- **Windows settings:** the links to the source code and open-source licenses each have their own line.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.5.1.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.5.1-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.5.1.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.5.1-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using 0.5.0?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Check now** (Windows) and choose **Download and install**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered.
