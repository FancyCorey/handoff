## What's changed

- **A new look that shows where your headphones are.** Each pair of headphones now has a small picture of your devices, with the headphones drawn above the one that has them. When they can come to this device, an arrow points here: tap **Move here** and watch them travel along it.
- **Clearer, shorter words everywhere.** Statuses are a few words ("On Studio PC", "Here"), and every problem says what happened and offers one button that fixes it. The longer explanation is one tap away under **More**.
- **Tidier home screen.** Your main headphones (the ones the Quick Settings tile moves) get the big picture; others get a compact card with their own **Move here**. Your linked devices are shown as a row with a dot when they're online.
- **Settings, simplified.** Choices like "When this phone is locked" and "When something starts playing here" are now side-by-side options with one sentence each. **Help and diagnostics** has moved into Settings.
- **Smaller download.** The Android app is about 4 MB instead of 50 MB.
- **Software bill of materials.** Each release now lists every library the apps contain (the `-sbom.cdx.json` files).

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.7.0.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.7.0-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.7.0.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.7.0-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Settings → Check** (Windows) and choose **Download and install** (on 0.6.x it's still called that). Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every app file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered.
