## What's changed

- **Privacy policy in the app.** **Settings → Privacy → Privacy policy** opens Handoff's [privacy policy](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/PRIVACY.md): what Handoff stores on your device, what it sends to your other devices, and what it never collects.
- **Edition shown in About.** Settings now shows which edition you have. This download is the **GitHub edition**: free, no ads, and it updates itself from GitHub. A Google Play edition with the same features is being prepared.
- **Steadier battery readings.** On phones that don't let apps read a headset's battery level directly, Handoff stops asking after the first try, instead of trying again every time a headset connects.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.6.0.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.6.0-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.6.0.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.6.0-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Check now** (Windows) and choose **Download and install**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered.
