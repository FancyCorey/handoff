## What's changed

- **Says when your devices aren't on the same network.** Devices on two different Wi-Fi networks can't reach each other, and Handoff used to just say the other device "didn't answer". It now says "Galaxy S22 is on a different Wi-Fi" (with a shortcut to hotspot settings) or "This phone isn't on Wi-Fi" (with a shortcut to Wi-Fi settings), on the headphones' card, after a failed move, and in **Help and diagnostics**.
- **A Bluetooth log for audio problems.** If your headphones crackle or cut out after a move, **Help and diagnostics → Technical details → Bluetooth log** now records, on each device, when the Bluetooth link to the headphones opened and closed, which audio profiles were connected, when audio started and stopped streaming, and which codec (for example AAC or SBC) was in use. **Share a report** includes it.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.7.3.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.7.3-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.7.3.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.7.3-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Settings → Check** (Windows) and choose **Update**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every app file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered. The `-sbom.cdx.json` files list every library the apps contain.
