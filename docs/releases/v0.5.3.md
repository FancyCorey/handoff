## What's changed

- **Choose background mode during setup.** The last setup step on Android now has a **Stay reachable in the background** switch. Turn it on so your other devices can take the headphones from this one even when Handoff is closed or the screen is locked. It stays off unless you switch it on.
- **Clearer advice when another device can't be reached.** The message now tells you to open Handoff on that device, or to turn on background mode there.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.5.3.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.5.3-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.5.3.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.5.3-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Check now** (Windows) and choose **Download and install**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are. If you installed Handoff before this version, check **Settings → Stay reachable in the background** on each phone and tablet.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered.
