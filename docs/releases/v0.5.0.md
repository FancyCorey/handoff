**Handoff moves your Bluetooth headphones between your Android phone, tablet and Windows PC with one tap.** Pick up the device you want to listen on, press **Move here**, and the headphones switch over. No Bluetooth menus, no re-pairing. It's free and open source, and it needs no account.

This is the first public release.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.5.0.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.5.0-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.5.0.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.5.0-windows-portable.zip** — unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

Install Handoff on every device you use your headphones with. You'll find step-by-step instructions in the [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md).

## What you can do

- **Move your headphones with one tap.** The device that has them lets go, the one in your hand connects, and Handoff checks that sound is really coming through.
- **See where your headphones are.** Every device shows which one has them, plus their battery level.
- **Use it anywhere.** Link your devices once and they find each other on any Wi-Fi. With no Wi-Fi around, a hotspot from your phone or PC does the job.
- **Change your mind.** You can cancel a move while it's happening, or step away and come back to follow its progress.
- **Get a clear answer when something's wrong.** If a move can't finish, Handoff tries once more by itself, then tells you what happened in plain words.
- **Keep it private.** No account, no cloud and no tracking. Your devices only talk to each other, encrypted, on your own network.
- **Stay up to date.** Handoff can check for new versions for you (**Settings → Check for updates**), and only installs updates that genuinely come from this project.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because this app is new and not yet code-signed. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- **Switching on Android** relies on features that some phone makers restrict. Handoff tells you in its first-run screen whether your phone supports it.
- Want to check a download hasn't been altered? `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every file.
