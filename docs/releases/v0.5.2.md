## What's changed

- **Linking works when another copy of Handoff is installed.** Devices now tell each other exactly where to reach them, so a second copy of Handoff on the same device (for example an older test version) no longer intercepts moves.
- **Warning about an older copy.** On Android, Handoff shows a notice on its home screen when an older test version is installed next to it, with a button to uninstall it.
- **The right device is named when a move fails.** If a linked device couldn't be reached, or doesn't recognise this one, Handoff says so and explains how to fix it, instead of reporting that the headphones didn't connect.
- **Offline devices show as offline.** A linked device that refuses the connection no longer appears as *Online*.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.5.2.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.5.2-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.5.2.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.5.2-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Check now** (Windows) and choose **Download and install**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered.
