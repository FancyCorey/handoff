## What's changed

- **A simpler home screen.** Back to the clear layout of 0.6: every pair of headphones gets the same card, with where they are, their battery and a full-width **Move here** at the bottom, and every linked device is listed underneath, including this one. The picture of your devices from 0.7.0 is gone from the home screen.
- **Moves happen right in the card.** Tap **Move here** and the card shows each step and a **Cancel** button, then "Moved here". You no longer jump to another screen; **Details** shows every step if you want them.
- **Problems are fixed where you see them.** If a move doesn't work, the card says what happened and shows the one button that fixes it, such as **Try again** or **Link again**.
- **Bluetooth off is one message, not one per card.** A banner at the top offers **Turn on**, and each pair of headphones says where it was last seen.
- **Under the hood:** newer Android libraries and build tools.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.7.1.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.7.1-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.7.1.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.7.1-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Settings → Check** (Windows) and choose **Update**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every app file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered. The `-sbom.cdx.json` files list every library the apps contain.
