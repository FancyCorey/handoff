## What's changed

- **Prefer SBC on Android.** If Bluetooth audio cuts out, open the headphones in Handoff and choose **Bluetooth audio → Prefer SBC**. Android asks you to allow access to that pair. Handoff waits for playback to pause before requesting a change and says when Android confirms SBC. Availability depends on the device's Bluetooth software.
- **Less Bluetooth activity during playback.** Background searches stop when music starts, and routine status updates skip the Bluetooth route while music plays. Moves can still find linked devices, and Handoff leaves the new audio connection in place after a successful move. When the old device is still connecting, Handoff waits for that connection attempt to end before confirming release.
- **More detail in shared reports.** The report shows the codec Android returned for each connected pair that Handoff can read. The Bluetooth log distinguishes Classic and LE events when Android identifies them, and shows when nearby advertising rotates or scanning starts and stops.

Update **both** devices if you use Handoff between them.
