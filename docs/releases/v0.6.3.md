## What's changed

- **No more audio cutting out with headphones that connect to two devices.** Headphones like these play whichever device last made a sound, so a notification or video on your tablet could interrupt music on your phone. **Move here** now disconnects the headphones from your other devices, so only the device you chose can play. If they end up connected to two devices anyway (many reconnect to both when switched on), Handoff says so and offers **Use only here**.
- **New option: Stay connected to other devices.** It replaces the old "Multipoint headset" switch and is off unless you turn it on. Turn it on only if you want your headphones connected to several devices at once.
- **Automatic switching is calmer.** It now waits until something has been playing for 2 seconds, so short sounds can't take the headphones.
- **New look.** A new icon (headphones whose left half is an arrow, handing the sound over) and matching colours on Android and Windows.

**Already using Handoff with headphones that connect to two devices?** If you had switched on **Multipoint headset**, open the headset in Handoff on each device and check that **Stay connected to other devices** is off.

## Which file do I need?

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Android phone or tablet (Android 12 or newer) | **Handoff-0.6.3.apk** |
| Windows 10 or 11 PC | **Handoff-0.6.3-setup.exe** (recommended) or **Handoff-0.6.3.msi** |
| Windows, without installing anything | **Handoff-0.6.3-windows-portable.zip**: unzip it and run `Handoff.exe` |

**Already using Handoff?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** (Android) or **Check now** (Windows) and choose **Download and install**. Your linked devices and headphones stay as they are.

New to Handoff? The [getting started guide](https://github.com/FancyCorey/handoff/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) walks through setup step by step.

## Good to know

- **Android** asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager the first time you open the APK.
- **Windows** may warn "Windows protected your PC" because Handoff isn't code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**. On PCs with *Smart App Control* switched on, Windows doesn't offer that option yet.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the fingerprint of every file, so you can check a download hasn't been altered.
