# Code of conduct

Handoff is a small project that welcomes everyone who wants to use it, report problems or help improve it, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here aren't developers; a question is never a nuisance.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@FancyCorey](https://github.com/FancyCorey)). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

This code applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
